# OASIS - Your guild. Your style. One place.

**One window for your whole guild: members, guild chat, chronicle, profiles, community and guild leadership - all in-game.**

OASIS is a guild hub built for **WoW Forever**. This is the **base add-on**. It works on its own, and you can add more areas with the optional OASIS add-ons below - install only what your guild needs.

## Add-ons for OASIS

| Add-on | What it adds |
|---|---|
| [**OASIS - Groups & Raids**](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/oasis-groups-raids) | Group finder, dungeon atlas, raid planner and sign-ups, loot master, soft reserve, raid check, buffs, tactics map, combat ranking, talents, rare spawns |
| [**OASIS - Trade**](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/oasis-trade) | Who crafts what, crafting orders, guild market and prices, guild wishlist |
| [**OASIS - Convenience**](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/oasis-convenience) | Personal helpers: auto repair/sell, quest helpers, item comparison, map extras, game chat, whisper log, upgrade search and more |

Every add-on needs this base add-on. Missing add-ons simply leave their area out - nothing breaks.

## A dashboard full of guild life

See who's online, follow level-ups and loot, check upcoming goals and discover your guild's **Hero of the Week**. The activity feed and the **chronicle** keep your guild's story in one place.

## Members, profiles & the PlayerBook

- **Member directory:** roster with ranks, alts, professions and last seen.
- **Profiles:** character pages with progress, badges, Oasis rank and a personal journey.
- **PlayerBook:** rate players you met, keep notes and share feedback with your guild - find great teammates again.

## Guild chat, emojis & sounds

Give guild conversations their own space: a guild chat with emojis, mentions and a history.
Your guild can build its **own emojis and sounds** in the free [Oasis Studio](https://oasisaddon.com/#studio) - right in the browser, nothing is uploaded. Guildmates install the same guild pack to see and hear them.

## Community & fun

- **Polls & guild goals:** decide together and work toward shared objectives.
- **Community:** profiles, birthdays, mentors for new members, thank-yous.
- **Guildotchi:** every member with Oasis has an animal on the guild meadow - feed it, pet it, keep it happy.
- **Deathroll:** a classic dice duel with a leaderboard; stakes are off by default.
- **Badges, collections & Legacy:** celebrate progress and milestones.
- **Campfires & deaths:** share campfires on the map and remember fallen heroes.

## Guild leadership

Recruiting, applicants, inactive members, attendance and announcements - tools for officers with access based on guild rank.

## Your guild, your look

Customize OASIS with **designs, colors, layouts, fonts and sounds**. Pin your favorite pages, reorder the sidebar and style your guild name's logo with a live preview.

## Your data, your choice

Optional guild data export lets you work with your own guild website or tools. **No OASIS-hosted service is required.** Data export is off by default and excludes officer notes, guild chat, whispers, player ratings, the blacklist, applicant data and personal settings.

## Available in 12 languages

English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Korean, Polish and Japanese. Translations are machine-assisted; feedback is welcome.

## Getting started

1. Unpack OASIS into your game's **Interface/AddOns** folder (plus any add-ons you want).
2. Restart the game and enable OASIS on the character selection screen.
3. Type **/oasis** or **/oa** to open the addon.
4. Pick your areas, language and look in the start window.

## Commands

- `/oasis` or `/oa` - open or close the window
- `/oa mini` - collapse to the slim bar
- `/oa sync` - sync guild data now
- `/oa demo` - fill Oasis with sample data to try it out (stays on your PC)
