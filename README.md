# OASIS - Your guild. Your style. One place.

**Plan raids, manage loot, find groups, and bring your guild to life - all in-game.**

OASIS is an all-in-one guild hub built for **WoW Forever**. It combines practical tools for guild leaders with features every member can use: a shared dashboard, PlayerBook, crafting, dungeon information, character progress, and a customizable interface.

## Raid planning & loot management

Organize raids with the built-in **Raid Planner**, coordinate your roster, track attendance, and prepare together. Raid checks help you review equipment and enchants before heading in.

Keep loot decisions in-game with **loot distribution tools, wishlists, and Soft Reserve**. Bring raid organization and member preferences into one place.

## PlayerBook - Remember who you played with

Rate players you've encountered in WoW Forever, keep notes about your experiences, and share player feedback with your guild.

- **Find great teammates again:** Remember the players you enjoyed grouping with.
- **Remember difficult encounters:** Keep track of players you would rather avoid.
- **Make future groups easier:** Use your ratings, notes, and shared guild feedback when choosing future teammates.

## Your guild, your look

Customize OASIS with **designs, colors, layouts, fonts, and sound settings**. Choose the look that suits you, pin your favorite pages, and personalize your guild name's logo style.

- **Logo customization:** Choose fonts, color styles, gradients, and letter spacing with a live preview.
- **Custom sounds:** Add your own audio through the optional **Oasis_Sounds** module.
- **Custom emojis:** Add your community's images through the optional **Oasis_Emojis** module.

Custom sound and emoji imports require additional setup outside the game. Guildmates need the same custom media modules to see your emojis and hear your shared sounds; media files are not transferred automatically through the addon. Animated custom emojis are supported. Private guild media and themed extras are not included in the public release.

## A dashboard full of guild life

See who's online, follow level-ups and loot, check upcoming goals, and discover your guild's **Hero of the Week**. The activity feed and chronicle make it easier to follow what your guild has been doing.

## Groups, crafting & shared resources

- **Group finder:** Find guildmates for dungeons and shared adventures.
- **Crafting marketplace:** Discover professions, recipes, and crafting options within your guild.
- **Guild bank:** View shared bank information and coordinate resources.
- **Member directory:** Browse character profiles, equipment, and professions.
- **Bags & alts:** Keep track of your characters and inventories.

## Dungeon & equipment tools

Explore the **dungeon atlas** with boss information, loot, quests, and drop chances. Use item comparisons, equipment information, and loot wishlists to plan your next upgrades.

## More than a roster

- **Guild chat & emojis:** Give guild conversations their own space.
- **Polls & guild goals:** Make decisions and work toward shared objectives.
- **Ranks, badges & collections:** Celebrate progress and milestones.
- **Legacy & guild graveyard:** Keep your guild's history close.
- **Combat rankings:** Explore your guild's combat leaderboard.
- **Deathroll:** A game to enjoy with your guildmates; stakes are disabled by default.
- **Recruitment & leadership:** Tools for guild officers and raid leaders, with access based on guild rank or authorized raid roles.

## Your data, your choice

Optional guild data export lets you work with your own guild website or tools. **No OASIS-hosted service is required.** Public data export is disabled by default and excludes officer notes, guild chat, whispers, player ratings, the blacklist, applicant data, and personal settings.

JSON export and Discord chat/event bridging use external companion tools and require your own setup. The addon itself does not connect directly to websites or Discord. Saved-data exports become available after logout or **/reload**; incoming Discord messages appear after login or reload. Imported Discord messages appear only in your local OASIS chat view; they are not relayed into native WoW chat or to other OASIS users. The Discord bridge requires external tools and is not a built-in Discord raid signup service.

## Available in 12 languages

English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Korean, Polish, and Japanese.

Translations are machine-assisted and may contain wording inconsistencies. Some game names remain in English. Translation feedback is welcome.

## Getting started

1. Install the public OASIS package in your game's **Interface/AddOns** folder.
2. Restart the game and enable OASIS on the character selection screen.
3. Type **/oasis** or **/oa** to open the addon.
4. Choose your language, appearance, and favorite pages.

## Public release & feedback

OASIS is actively being developed for **WoW Forever**. Compatibility with other game versions is not claimed. The public package excludes the private guild module and developer mode. Leadership features respect guild and raid permissions.

Report bugs, suggest features, and share translation feedback through our community channels. OASIS is released under **GPL-2.0**; third-party credits and license information are included with the addon.

## Commands

- `/oasis` or `/oa` - open or close the window
- `/oa mini` - collapse to the slim bar
- `/oa sync` - sync guild data now
- `/oa rundgang` - show the first-start tour again
- `/oa weiterleiten` - switch the whisper offer for alts on or off
- `/oa demo` - fill Oasis with sample data to try it out (stays on your PC)
